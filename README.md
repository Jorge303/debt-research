Regarding "FED model"
Trying to find variables that predict the FED funds rate decision.

Very unsurprisingly the variables regarding inflation rate and labor market seem to be the most statistically significant.
This model was not able to predict the decision of the individual FED governors by measuring the economic conditions in their respective jurisdictions. This is in line with the mandate that the governors should make the decision with the national level in mind and not just to represent the economic reality of their own jurisdiction.
